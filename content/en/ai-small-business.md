When you run a small business, time is the thing you're always short of. You serve customers, place orders, keep the books and still have to post on social media. Artificial intelligence won't run your business for you, but it can take a lot of repetitive tasks off your plate. Here are ten realistic uses, with no big investment, each with a prompt to get started.

## 1. Answering frequently asked questions

If people ask you the same thing a hundred times (opening hours, prices, parking, how to book), write clear answers once and reuse them.

```prompt
I run a [type of business] in [city]. These are the questions I'm asked most: [list]. My details: opening hours [hours], prices [prices], how to book [method]. Write a short, friendly answer to each question, ready to paste into WhatsApp or my website.
```

You can take this further with [quick replies in WhatsApp Business](guia:whatsapp).

## 2. Replying to Google reviews

Replying to reviews, good and bad, improves your business's image and shows you listen. With AI it takes a minute per review. We go through it step by step in [how to reply to reviews with AI](guia:reviews).

## 3. Creating social media content

Post ideas, captions, monthly calendars and short video scripts.

```prompt
I run a [type of business] and post on Instagram. Give me a calendar of 12 posts for next month: 4 showing the product, 4 showing the day-to-day of the business and 4 answering customer questions. For each one: idea, format and short caption.
```

There's more in [how to create Instagram content with AI](guia:instagram) and [scripts for short videos](guia:video-scripts).

## 4. Writing product and service descriptions

Product pages for your online shop, your restaurant menu or descriptions of your services.

```prompt
Write an 80-word description of this product for my online shop: [product, materials, size, who it's for]. Highlight the main benefit, make it easy to read on a phone and end with a sentence that invites people to buy. Friendly tone.
```

## 5. Preparing quotes and proposals

Many freelancers lose hours writing quotes. AI helps you create a clear template that you then adapt.

```prompt
I'm a [profession]. Create a professional quote template with: a short introduction, a description of the work by phases, what's included and what isn't, timelines, a price breakdown as [PRICE], payment terms and how long the quote is valid. Professional, clear tone.
```

>! **Careful:** AI doesn't know your costs or the tax rules that apply to you. Check prices, taxes and terms with your accountant.

## 6. Writing emails to customers and suppliers

Complaints, payment reminders, notices about changes to hours or prices. For the trickiest ones, follow [the guide to difficult emails](guia:emails).

## 7. Analysing feedback and spotting improvements

Paste several reviews or customer comments (without names) and ask for an analysis:

```prompt
Here are 30 customer reviews of my business. Group them by theme (service, price, quality, waiting times…), say which themes come up most positively and negatively, and suggest 3 specific, low-cost improvements I could make this month.

[paste the reviews without names]
```

## 8. Creating or improving your website

You can generate copy for your website, ideas for its structure, or even build a simple page with tools that turn a description into a site. We cover this in [how to build a website with AI](guia:website).

## 9. Training your team

Welcome guides, procedures (opening, closing, cleaning) or phone scripts.

```prompt
Create an opening procedure for my [type of business] as a checklist, with the tasks in order and the approximate time for each. Make it easy to follow for someone starting this week.
```

## 10. Spreadsheets and numbers

Formulas to track sales, expenses or stock without being an Excel expert. There are examples in [how to create Excel formulas with AI](guia:excel).

## Where to start

Don't try all ten ideas at once. Pick the task that eats the most time each week and start there. A good plan:

1. **Week 1:** prepare your answers to frequently asked questions.
2. **Week 2:** reply to all your pending reviews.
3. **Week 3:** create next month's social media calendar.
4. **Week 4:** look at what saved you the most time and repeat it.

> **Tip:** keep your best prompts in a document with your details already filled in (opening hours, tone, type of customer). That way you only change the specifics each time instead of starting from scratch.

## What you shouldn't hand over to AI

- **Important decisions** about pricing, hiring or investments without checking the numbers yourself.
- **Customer data:** don't paste customer lists, phone numbers or histories into unapproved tools. More in [what not to share with an AI](guia:privacy).
- **The personal touch.** AI helps you write, but what makes a small business special is the human connection. Review every message so it sounds like you.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to use AI in a small business?

You can start for free with the free plans of the main assistants. If you use them daily, an individual paid plan typically costs around €20 a month. Check current prices on each tool's website.

### Do I need technical skills?

No. For everything in this guide, you just need to be able to describe what you want. If you want to automate more complex processes, such as a chatbot connected to your website, professional help may be worth it.

### Can AI handle my customers on its own?

It can answer frequently asked questions, but it's best to always have a person reviewing or available for anything out of the ordinary. An angry customer handled only by a machine usually gets angrier.
