For many small businesses, WhatsApp is the main customer service channel: customers ask about prices, book appointments, confirm orders or complain. Replying quickly and well makes a difference, but it's impossible to watch your phone all day. The WhatsApp Business app includes tools to automate part of the work, and AI helps you fill them with good messages. In this guide you'll see how to set everything up in an afternoon.

## What WhatsApp Business offers

WhatsApp Business is the free version of WhatsApp for companies. Its usual features include:

- **Business profile:** address, opening hours, description, website.
- **Greeting message:** sent automatically when someone messages you for the first time or after a period of inactivity.
- **Away message:** sent outside your opening hours.
- **Quick replies:** saved texts you insert with a shortcut, for example by typing "/prices".
- **Labels:** to organise conversations (new customer, pending order, paid).
- **Catalogue:** to show products or services with a photo and price.

Available features may change with app updates. Check the business tools menu in your version.

## Step 1: collect the questions you're asked most

Look through your chats from the last few weeks and note the ten most repeated questions. They're usually variations of:

- What are your opening hours?
- How much does … cost?
- Do you have availability for…?
- Where are you? Is there parking?
- Do you deliver? How long does it take?
- How can I pay?

## Step 2: create all the replies at once

With the list in hand, ask the AI to write every quick reply in your tone:

```prompt
I run a [type of business] in [city]. Write WhatsApp Business quick replies to these frequently asked questions: [list]. My details: opening hours [hours], prices [prices], location [address and landmarks], payment methods [methods], delivery [terms]. Requirements: no more than 50 words per reply, friendly tone, at most one emoji per message, and end with a question that moves things forward (for example, "Shall I book you in for Tuesday?"). Suggest a short shortcut for each reply.
```

Copy each reply into the app's quick replies section with its shortcut.

> **Tip:** the last sentence of each reply matters most. A question like "Would you like me to book you in?" turns an enquiry into an appointment far more often than a simple "Best regards".

## Step 3: greeting and away messages

Almost everyone sees these two messages, so they're worth getting right.

```prompt
Write for my [business]'s WhatsApp Business:
1. A greeting message of no more than 45 words that says hello, explains how we can help and how to book or order.
2. An away message of no more than 40 words for outside opening hours, stating when we're available and when we'll reply.
[Friendly/professional] tone.
```

## Step 4: catalogue descriptions

If you sell products or services with fixed prices, the catalogue saves you a lot of questions.

```prompt
Write descriptions for my WhatsApp Business catalogue for these products: [list with features and prices]. No more than 30 words each, starting with the main benefit. Also suggest a short, clear name for each product.
```

## Step 5: messages for key moments

Also prepare templates for recurring situations: appointment confirmation, day-before reminder, order shipped, review request and follow-up after the service.

```prompt
Write 5 WhatsApp templates for my [business]: appointment confirmation, reminder the day before, order shipped notice, Google review request after the service and a follow-up message one week later. Use [NAME], [DATE], [TIME] and [LINK] where needed. No more than 40 words each.
```

## Replying to tricky messages

For complaints or difficult messages, use AI to help draft, but reply yourself. Describe the situation without personal data:

```prompt
A customer has messaged me on WhatsApp upset because [situation]. I want to apologise once, explain the solution ([solution]) and keep the good relationship. Write a short, human WhatsApp reply of no more than 60 words.
```

There are more examples in [how to write difficult emails with AI](guia:emails), which work just as well for messages.

>! **Careful:** don't paste whole conversations with customers' names, phone numbers or health details into an AI tool. Describe the situation in your own words. If your business handles sensitive data, such as a clinic, check with your adviser what you're allowed to do.

## What about a chatbot that replies on its own?

There are solutions that connect an AI assistant to WhatsApp to reply automatically around the clock, using WhatsApp's official business platform. They can be useful for businesses with many messages, but they cost money, need setting up and should be supervised. Before taking that step:

1. Start with quick replies and automatic messages for a few weeks.
2. Measure how many messages you receive and which ones repeat.
3. If the volume justifies it, consider an automated solution from a provider that uses the official platform and always leaves the option to talk to a person.

## FAQ

### Is WhatsApp Business free?

The WhatsApp Business app is free. Some advanced features and the platform for automated integrations may have costs. Check the current terms on WhatsApp's official website.

### Can I use my current number?

Yes, you can move your number to WhatsApp Business, but back up your chats first. Many businesses prefer a separate number to keep personal and work life apart.

### Do automatic replies feel cold?

It depends on how they're written. With a friendly tone, short messages and a closing question that invites a reply, most customers see them as fast service, not as cold.
