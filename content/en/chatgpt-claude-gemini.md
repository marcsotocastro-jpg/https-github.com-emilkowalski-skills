ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are the best-known artificial intelligence assistants, and all three are very capable. The question of which one is "best" has no single answer: it depends on what you use it for, the tools you already have and how you like to work. In this guide we compare their general strengths and suggest a ten-minute test so you can decide with your own tasks.

>! **Careful:** these tools are updated constantly. They release new models, change features and adjust their plans and prices. Treat what you read here as general guidance and always check current features on each tool's official website.

## What they have in common

Before looking at differences, it helps to know that all three:

- Write, summarise, translate and explain topics to a very high standard.
- Work well in many languages.
- Have a free plan with limits and paid plans with more usage and features.
- Can work with files and images, with differences depending on the plan.
- Have mobile apps.
- Can make mistakes or invent facts, so all of them need checking.

For everyday tasks (an email, a summary, an explanation), any of the three will serve you well.

## Their general strengths

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)

The most popular and one of the most versatile. It stands out for how many features it brings together in one place: conversation, image generation, voice mode, file analysis and web search, among others. A good choice if you want an all-in-one tool and like trying new features.

### Claude (Anthropic)

It stands out for the quality of its writing, which tends to sound natural, and for working well with long texts and documents. Many people prefer it for drafting, editing, analysing documents and coding. A good choice if you write a lot or work with long documents.

### Gemini (Google)

Its big advantage is integration with Google services: Gmail, Docs, Drive and Search, depending on your plan and settings. A good choice if you already work in Google's tools and want AI inside them.

## Quick comparison

| If you mostly need… | Try first |
|---|---|
| A tool with many features in one place | ChatGPT |
| Writing and editing with a natural style | Claude |
| Working with long documents | Claude |
| Using AI inside Gmail, Docs or Drive | Gemini |
| Generating images in the same conversation | ChatGPT or Gemini |
| Research with cited sources | Any of them with web search on, or a search engine like Perplexity |

This table sums up general tendencies. Your experience with your own tasks is what should decide.

## The ten-minute test

The best way to choose is to try all three with the tasks you actually do. Open them in separate tabs and paste these prompts into each:

**Test 1: writing**

```prompt
Write an email to an online shop asking for a refund for an order that arrived faulty 10 days ago. I've already written once and had no reply. Firm, polite tone, no more than 120 words.
```

**Test 2: explaining**

```prompt
Explain what inflation is and how it affects my savings, as if I knew nothing about economics. Use an example with simple numbers and finish with 3 practical tips.
```

**Test 3: your real task**

Ask for something you do often at work or in your studies: summarise a document (without private data), prepare a lesson, create a social media calendar or edit a text.

**How to score:** rate each answer from 1 to 5 on:

- **Clarity:** is it easy to understand first time?
- **Usefulness:** could you use it almost unchanged?
- **Tone:** does it sound natural or like a template?
- **Accuracy:** are there errors or doubtful facts?

Add up the points. The winner for you is the one that scores highest on your real task, not on the general tests.

> **Tip:** you don't have to pick just one. Many people use two: one as their main tool and another to cross-check important answers or for specific tasks where it does better.

## Is it worth paying?

Always start with the free plans. Paying makes sense when:

- You often hit the free plan's message limit.
- You need features only on the paid plan, such as analysing many files or using the most advanced models more often.
- You use it for work and the time saved justifies the price.

Individual paid plans typically cost around €20 a month. There's an approximate table on our [tools](page:tools) page. Before paying, check whether your company or school provides access to one of them.

## Privacy: compare this too

Besides answer quality, check in each tool:

- Whether it uses your conversations to train its models and how to switch that off.
- How long it keeps your conversations.
- Whether your company allows it with work information.

## FAQ

### Which AI is the smartest?

The three keep overtaking each other with every update, and technical benchmarks change every few months. For daily use, the differences matter less than knowing how to ask well. Start with [how to write a good prompt](guia:prompt).

### Can I use all three for free?

Yes, all three have free plans with usage limits. For most people, the free plan of one or two of them is enough to start.

### Which is best in languages other than English?

All three work very well in the major languages. If you notice differences, they tend to be in writing style rather than understanding. Run the test in this guide and choose the one that sounds most natural to you.
